<h2 align="center"><a href="The-Ruins-of-Bezan">The Ruins of Bezan</a></h2>

<p align="center">A rain-soaked 3D exploration game in C and OpenGL 4.5.</p>

Wander torchlit ruins and a palace of talking characters, then head out to a chalky turquoise coast: a pastel fishing village, a beach shop that takes shells as money, whistling walruses, fishing and rowing boats, a volcano that erupts every thirty minutes, tornadoes and hail, and a palace on the sea floor.
