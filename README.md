### [The Ruins of Bezan](The-Ruins-of-Bezan)

A rain-soaked 3D exploration game in C and OpenGL 4.5. Wander torchlit ruins and a talking palace, then out to a
chalky turquoise coast: a pastel fishing village, a shell-money beach shop, whistling walruses, fishing and boats,
a volcano that erupts every 30 minutes, tornadoes and hail, and a palace on the sea floor.
